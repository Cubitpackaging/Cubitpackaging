# Cubit Packaging LLC

**Custom packaging, designed with AI and made to order.**

[Cubit Packaging](https://www.cubitpackaging.com) (**Cubit Packaging LLC**) is a US-based custom packaging manufacturer and software platform headquartered in **Fremont, California**. Working through our team and a network of more than 10 certified partner facilities, we supply custom printed packaging to brands across the United States — from 250-unit launches to 150,000+ unit repeat corporate programs.
