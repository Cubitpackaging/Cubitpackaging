# Cubit Packaging LLC

**Custom packaging, designed with AI and made to order.**

[Cubit Packaging](https://www.cubitpackaging.com) (**Cubit Packaging LLC**) is a US-based custom packaging manufacturer and software platform headquartered at **4425 Technology Dr, Fremont, CA 94538**. We combine custom packaging production (from 250 units to 150,000+ repeat corporate runs) with **7 integrated packaging software modules** and **11 free interactive engineering & design tools** so brands can design, engineer, order, track, and verify packaging in one place.
